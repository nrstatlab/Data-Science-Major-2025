Data Science Major 2025

B.Sc. (Hons) Data Science — Model Syllabus, w.e.f. AY 2025-26

Nineteen courses, unit by unit, with 358 lab source files behind them — every numeric claim on this site was produced by running code.

Click any course tile to open its full study material. All ten Major courses of Semesters I–IV and all five of Semester V are complete — fifteen courses, both elective tracks. Semester VI is listed by title and credits only in the source document.

⚠ Four topics are examined but appear in no syllabus unit

Bayes' theorem — Statistics Unit 1 lists only "conditional probability", yet Bayes appears in the prescribed activities quiz and in lab experiment 2. Covered here →

Database triggers — DBMS Unit 5 lists procedures and functions with no mention of triggers, yet the course objective names them and two of the six PL/SQL lab questions are trigger problems. Covered here →

GridFS and transactions — Document Database Unit 5 ends at replication and mentions neither, yet course outcome 4 names both and lab experiments 18 and 19 are exactly those two problems. Covered here →

Thirteen further findings are in the syllabus review, including damaged bibliographies in all five Semester III–IV courses.

Year I — Semester I

Course 1Core

Computer Fundamentals & Office Automation

5 Units + Lab • Number systems, memory hierarchy, networks, Word, Excel, pivot tables and dashboards.

 Course 2Core

Problem Solving Using C

5 Units + Lab • Control flow, arrays, strings, pointers, structures and file handling. 15 runnable programs.

Year I — Semester II

Course 3Core

Python Programming & Data Structures

5 Units + Lab • Syntax, collections, files, exceptions, OOP, linked lists, stacks, queues. 18 runnable programs.

 Course 4Core

Statistical Foundations for Data Science

5 Units + Lab • Probability, distributions, correlation, regression, estimation and hypothesis testing. Formula sheet included.

Year II — Semester III

Course 5Core

Database Management Systems

5 Units + Lab • Three-schema architecture, ER modelling, normalization to 3NF, SQL joins and PL/SQL. Executable SQL labs.

 Course 6Core

Data Science with R

5 Units + Lab • The data science lifecycle, R and RStudio, dplyr and ggplot2, regression and clustering, ARIMA and Shiny. 18 R scripts, 14 with executed Python equivalents.

 Course 7Core

Web Technologies

5 Units + Lab • HTML structure and forms, CSS layout and responsive design, JavaScript and the DOM, client-side validation, JSON and jQuery. 16 experiments, 184 assertions under jsdom.

Year II — Semester IV

Course 8Core

Data Mining

5 Units + Lab • Data warehousing and OLAP, preprocessing and similarity, Apriori and FP-Growth, ID3 and C4.5, K-Means and DBSCAN. Every hand trace re-executed in scikit-learn.

 Course 9Core

Python for Data Analysis & Visualization

5 Units + Lab • NumPy arrays and broadcasting, Pandas Series and DataFrames, cleaning and feature engineering, reshaping and merging, matplotlib, Seaborn and Plotly. All 18 practicals run.

 Course 10Core

Document Oriented Database

5 Units + Lab • NoSQL and CAP, the BSON document model, CRUD and MQL, embed against reference, aggregation pipelines, indexing and replication. Every query executed through mongomock.

Year III — Semester V

Course 11, plus a pair of electives from one domain — both tracks are covered here

Course 11Core

Business Intelligence Tools

5 Units + Lab • BI concepts and the data warehouse, Power BI and Power Query, DAX, Tableau and LOD expressions, dashboard design. Every figure computed, tool click-paths marked not executed.

 Course 12 ATrack A

Machine Learning

5 Units + Lab • ML paradigms, preprocessing and evaluation, regression, classification and clustering. All 12 practicals run under scikit-learn.

 Course 13 ATrack A

Artificial Intelligence

5 Units + Lab • Intelligent agents, uninformed and informed search, CSPs, propositional and first-order logic, expert systems. 19 Prolog experiments, five running as real logic programs.

 Course 12 BTrack B

Big Data Technologies

5 Units + Lab • HDFS and YARN, MapReduce, Hive and Pig, ingestion and serialization, HBase, ZooKeeper and Spark. 14 of 17 practicals run, including real Apache Spark, Avro and Parquet.

 Course 13 BTrack B

Cloud Computing for Data Science

5 Units + Lab • Service and deployment models, virtualization, cloud storage and warehouses, managed ML, deployment and monitoring. IAM evaluation, a real ETL and a real endpoint, all run locally.

You must stay in the same domain across both Semester V and VI, so you take either 12 A and 13 A or 12 B and 13 B. Both tracks are written out in full here. See the syllabus map.

Year III — Semester VI

The second half of your chosen track

Course 14 ATrack A

Neural Networks and Deep Learning

5 Units + Lab • Perceptrons and activations, backpropagation, CNNs, RNNs and LSTM, attention and transformers, transfer learning and AI ethics. Ten of twelve experiments run against real MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, IMDb and real ImageNet weights.

 Course 15 ATrack A

Natural Language Processing

5 Units + Lab • Ambiguity and regular expressions, tokenization, stemming and lemmatization, grammars and parsing, named entity recognition, embeddings, classification, RNNs and transformers. Eleven of fourteen experiments run against real NLTK corpora and real spaCy models.

 Course 14 BTrack B

Time Series Analysis and Forecasting

5 Units + Lab • Stationarity, ACF and PACF, ARMA, ARIMA and SARIMA, model selection, prediction intervals, VAR and Granger causality, Kalman filtering and spectral analysis. Every one of the 13 experiments runs.

 Course 15 BTrack B

Data Engineering and MLOps

5 Units + Lab • The data engineering lifecycle, architecture and distributed systems, MLOps fundamentals, deployment and CI/CD, monitoring and governance. Eleven of sixteen experiments run against real MLflow, git, DVC and Flask.
